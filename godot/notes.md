## brainstorming
what STATS should there be ?
- boss satisfaction 🧑‍💼
- money $
- customer satisfaction 😁

has to keep each of them above a threshold to not get fired - as they make
decisions thru out the day, maybe they get feedback like seeing **$++** when
they approve an expensive drink etc

what do we add next ?

break the machine and make you wait more while it resets ?)

like ice, sugar, milk from the side and add them to the drinks)
